# Code Primates: SPOILERS

**Stop here if you want to solve the game yourself.** This is the complete walkthrough, every code, and a list
of the hidden gags. (The game's source code also contains the solutions, as the tests in `tools/` play the whole
game. If you want to avoid spoilers, don't browse `scripts/`, `assets/dialogue/` or `tools/`.)

Press **N** in the game for your Notes: the clues you have found so far are listed there.

---

## The short version

1. Get the **coffee** and drink it. (The monkey can't use any computer without it.)
2. Get the **IT lab keycard** from your desk drawer, then work through the **basement** to the **server room**.
3. On the **3rd floor**, get the **accountant's PIN** and the **CEO's key**.
4. In the **CEO's office**, open the safe, take the silver golf ball, and sink the putt for the **USB stick**.
5. Copy the deploy package from the **server room terminal**, then run it on the **CEO's terminal**.

## Full walkthrough

### The office (2nd floor): where you start
- Take the **mug** and the **paperclip** from your desk.
- Use the **paperclip** on the **drawer** to get the **keycard** (it opens the IT lab).
- Leave through the door, then take the elevator (the door in the hallway).

### Coffee (ground floor)
- Take the elevator to **G**, go right to the lobby, and through the door to the **break room**.
- **Take the carafe** from the coffee machine, **use it on the water cooler**, then **use the full carafe on the
  coffee machine**. It brews.
- **Take a paper cup** and **use it on the machine**, or use **your mug** on the machine. You get a **cup of coffee**.
- **Use the coffee on the monkey** (click him while holding it). He drinks it, his tail straightens, and he can now use computers.
- Shortcut: **water into the coffee machine** works too. Use your mug or a cup of water on the machine and it makes
  coffee straight away, so you can never get stuck.

### The plant and the coin (ground floor)
- Fill a **paper cup** at the water cooler, and **use it on the dead plant** in the lobby. You get a **coin**.
- **Use the coin on the vending machine** in the break room for **banana chips**. They're only a gag. (Give them to
  the accountant, or eat them yourself by using them on the monkey.)

### Basement: IT lab, closet, server room
- Take the elevator to **B**. Use the **keycard** on the **IT lab door** (its light turns green).
- In the IT lab, take the **janitor's key** from the workbench (under the keyboard).
- Use the key on the **janitor closet** door in the hallway. Take the **flashlight** and the **spare fuse**.
- In the IT lab: use the **fuse** on the **fuse box**. The manager's office lights up.
- Use the **flashlight** on the **server rack** to read the serial: **XR-7-5189**. (The sticky note on the bench says the
  password is the last four digits of the serial: **5189**.)
- In the **IT manager's office** (right of the rack), use the **serial note** on the **computer** (you need the coffee).
  It unlocks the **desk drawer**: take the **green keycard**.
- Use the **green keycard** on the green **server room door** in the IT lab. (It leads to Dale, the deploy
  terminal, and the server racks.)

### 3rd floor: the accountant and the VP
- Take the elevator to **3**.
- **Accounting** (first door): the accountant is counting beans, one every 3 seconds. **Give them a coffee** (use your
  mug of coffee or a cup of coffee on them), then **talk to them**. They lose count and mutter their PIN: **4719**. You get
  the **PIN note**. (They keep your mug if you give it to them: "What a nice coffee mug!")
- **VP office** (second door): take the **CEO's key** from the **keyring on the wall hook**.
- Use the **CEO's key** on the **CEO's office** door (third door).

### The CEO's office
- Click the **golf picture** to swing it aside. There's a **safe** behind it.
- Use the **PIN note** on the safe (**4719**) for the **silver golf ball**.
- Use the **silver golf ball** on the **putter** or the **golf cup**. The monkey fetches the putter, takes two practice
  swings, and sinks it. The cup pops up the **USB stick**.

### The ending
- Go back to the **basement server room**. Use the **USB stick** on the **deploy terminal**. It copies the deploy package.
- Take the stick to the **CEO's terminal** (3rd floor) and use it there. That ends the game.

## Codes and numbers

| What | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Boss's door code | **3141** | Sticky note under the conference room table |
| Accountant's PIN (CEO's safe) | **4719** | The accountant, after a coffee |
| Rack serial / manager's password | **XR-7-5189** / **5189** | Server rack in the IT lab (needs the flashlight) |

## Optional rooms and hints
- **Boss's office** (2nd floor, the keypad door): enter **3141**. Nothing is needed from in here, but the desk drawer
  has a note: "Safe PIN? Ask Accounting." The trophies, the desk's nameplate and the chair have gags too.
- **Conference room**: the speakerphone, the memo (about memos), and the sticky note with the door code.
- **Dale** (server room): he'll hint that the terminal wants a USB stick. "It's technically Saturday in Tokyo" gets a smile.

## Hidden gags
- **Giant plant:** water the plant a **second time** (or pour a whole **carafe of water** on it). It shoots up to
  the ceiling for a few minutes, and the **receptionist** appears: "What did you do to my plant?!" Talk to the plant: "I'm fine, thanks."
- **Talk to:** the microwave, the VP's phone (a voicemail menu), the speakerphone, the vending machine, Dale, the trophies, the boss's desk nameplate.
- **Filing cabinets** and shelves are full of TPS reports. Take one: "It needs a new cover sheet."
- **Mug of water:** the monkey won't drink water, but pour it into the coffee machine and it becomes coffee.
- **Elevator:** read the signs. Look at the exit door in the break room, too.
- **The credits:** the ending has a credits roll. The Notes screen (N) lists every clue as you find it.

## If you get stuck
- Can't use a computer? **Drink a coffee** first (use a cup or mug of coffee on the monkey).
- The IT lab won't open? You need the **keycard** from the office desk drawer (use the paperclip on the drawer).
- The machine won't brew? It needs **water**. Fill the carafe at the water cooler, then use it on the machine.
- The accountant won't talk? They need **coffee** first.
